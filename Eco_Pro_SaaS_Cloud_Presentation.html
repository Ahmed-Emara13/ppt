First, what is SaaS?

Software as a Service: you use it, someone else runs it.

Like electricity

Nobody builds a power station at home. You plug in and pay every month. SaaS does the same for software: no server, no installation, just a subscription.

The customer

Just uses it

  • Chooses a plan, pays monthly or yearly
  • Opens it in a browser, on a laptop or a phone
  • Adds apps and users as the business grows

The provider · Eco Pro

Runs everything

  • Servers, installation and updates
  • Security and a padlock on every address
  • Backups and monitoring, day and night

You already use SaaS every day: Gmail, Microsoft 365, Zoom. Eco Pro SaaS Cloud brings the same idea to a complete ERP, with Eco Pro's own apps inside.
